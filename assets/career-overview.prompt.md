# Career overview artwork and typography

用途：README概念说明图，说明职业发展框架；不是实际界面截图。人物和场景由AI生成，不对应使用者的照片或职业经历。

原始拼贴由内置image_gen生成，再按用户要求移除右侧人手。中文文案通过可编辑SVG独立排版；后续仅精简关键词与调整行距，没有用程序重绘位图。SVG画布为2400×1600，内嵌原生1536×1024 PNG，不依赖外部图片。系统字体不随包分发，跨平台排字可能略有不同。此概念图标签为中文；五语言README均在正文解释功能，图片alt使用相应语言。

参考[Anchor原作](https://www.wemeanthis.com/work/anchor)、[Figma/COLLINS原作](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/figma/)与[Readymag说明](https://blog.readymag.com/readymag-marketing-design-philosophy/)的拼贴/编排方法；未复制这些项目的图像、字体或标志。生成图与项目原创排版按本包MIT发布。

## Original generation prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing / illustration-story. Create one ORIGINAL avant-garde editorial PHOTOMONTAGE / cut-paper collage artwork for a refined personal career-development tool. This is a text-free key visual for a poster; a designer will typeset all words afterward. There must be absolutely NO typography, NO lettering, NO logos, NO labels, NO numerals anywhere.

Art direction: a highly art-directed contemporary cultural magazine cover or independent design studio poster, sophisticated surreal analog collage, subtle printed-paper grain, precise photographic cutouts, mostly flat rather than a 3D product render. An airy almost-white, very faint cool grey-blue paper background, charcoal/black-and-white photographic material with small dusty sky-blue accents. A tiny warm orange pencil stroke may appear once. This should feel intelligent, human and quietly experimental, consistent with a minimal white productivity workspace, not a corporate stock illustration.

Composition: wide landscape approximately 3:2. Keep the LEFT 42 percent of the entire canvas almost entirely EMPTY white/very pale grey-blue paper for a large editorial headline that will be added later. Keep the top 15 percent quiet for a brand line and keep the bottom 17 percent quiet for a short caption and navigation labels. Concentrate a SINGLE memorable collage composition in the center-right, with an irregular hand-cut silhouette and ample breathing room around it. Do not divide the visual into three boxes or three separate scenes.

Main visual metaphor: a person can assemble a new direction from fragments of experience, and still choose a different future. A lightly halftoned black-and-white cutout of an anonymous small walking figure, seen from the back, steps along an unexpected ribbon of torn paper and archival fragments toward a large open rectangular outline/frame. Within that frame is an airy pale-blue slice of sky/horizon, not a tropical postcard. The walking figure is modest in scale and has ordinary contemporary clothing; no recognizable face. Collage a cropped black-and-white hand into the scene as if arranging one fragment of the path, conveying personal agency and thoughtful curation. The paper fragments may contain subtle ruled lines, blank margins, a few abstract handwritten marks and a single outlined circle/checkpoint, but NO readable text. A fine continuous graphite or muted blue line threads through the torn fragments and continues beyond the open frame, suggesting ongoing dialogue, action and feedback. Use carefully cropped geometric paper planes, a soft blue translucent vellum patch and a restrained irregular circular cutout to create avant-garde tension. Restrict to a few strong pieces; leave deliberate gaps and asymmetry. This is a meaningful, coherent photomontage with one focal point.

Surface: tactile matte paper and printed photographs, clean cut edges mixed with a few authentic torn edges, subtle imperfect registration only in small areas; almost no cast shadow except a whisper between paper layers. Strong visual proportions, editorial intelligence, calm negative space. Avoid decorative clutter or distressed dirty yellow paper.

Strict avoid: glossy 3D, isometric office supplies, floating file-folder icons, glass or marble podiums, clay icons, cute mascots, stock teamwork cartoons, glowing AI orbs, giant arrows, three-column infographic, dramatic sunsets, mountains-and-success inspirational stock imagery, saturated rainbow palette, neon gradients, simulated app interface, tiny fake text, motivational lettering, generated Chinese characters, watermarks. No text whatsoever. This must look like a fresh experimental collage from an independent art director, not a generic AI infographic.
```

## Hand-removal edit prompt

```text
Edit the referenced original collage artwork. Make ONE precise change: completely REMOVE the large photographic human hand, fingers and dark sleeve entering from the lower-right edge, including its cast shadow. Fill the removed hand area naturally with the existing very pale cool grey-blue paper background and, where necessary, a continuation of the existing flat torn-paper collage layers beneath it. The small warm orange pencil stroke on the paper may remain only if it still feels natural; do not invent a new hand or any replacement object.

Preserve everything else as closely as possible: the 1536x1024 landscape composition, the huge empty left portion for later typography, the walking black-and-white figure seen from the back near the open sky frame, the open rectangular frame and pale-blue sky, the continuous thin blue line, all existing torn paper, grid paper, rocks and photographic fragments, their positions and scale, the subtle paper texture, restrained charcoal and dusty blue palette, flat avant-garde editorial photomontage style and ample negative space. Keep the walking person: the user is removing the separate oversized hand, NOT the person. Do not redesign the composition or add new collage objects. No glossy 3D objects, no folders, no arrows, no typography, no logos, no letters, no labels, no watermarks. This remains a text-free artwork; all real text will be typeset separately after the edit.
```
